# Murshid Developer — basic community rules

1. Upload only media you created or have permission to distribute.
2. Do not post personal information, threats, harassment, scams, or illegal content.
3. Respect copyright, privacy, and local laws.
4. Do not impersonate another creator.
5. Administrators may restrict videos or block accounts that violate these rules.
6. This starter project does not provide a formal reporting queue or legal takedown workflow. Add those before public launch.
